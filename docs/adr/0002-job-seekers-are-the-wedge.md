# 2. Job seekers are the wedge — accepted 2026-08-28

## Decision

v1 serves one buyer: a job seeker reading an offer letter, non-compete or IP
assignment before signing. Tenants, freelancers and small businesses are not
served. Redline operates before signature only. The saved library is deferred.

## Alternatives

- **Tenants.** Largest population and a strong price anchor ($450–$660 flat).
  Rejected: the research found no first-person tenant account at all, and a
  tenant on a standard lease has no leverage, which makes the counter-offer
  theatre.
- **Freelancers.** Real negotiating power and recurring use, which would have
  justified the library. Rejected: lowest budget in the research and only
  Medium evidence confidence.
- **A general contract reader serving all four document types.** Rejected: the
  research is explicit that four document types point at four buyers with
  different urgency, clause sets and distribution.
- **Chasing the after-signature pain** (auto-renewal, cancellation). Rejected as
  a different product, though it carries the strongest evidence in the research.

## Why

A reader of the brief can check this against the research directly: job seekers
carry High confidence, a one-time deadline-driven purchase, and the only
sourced per-document price for exactly this work ($542.59 average for a
non-compete review). It is also the only segment where every capability in
scope does real work — an offer letter is negotiable, so the counter-offer has
something to act on.

## Consequences

- The best-documented harm in the research (auto-renewal, ranked #1 by
  evidence) is one we barely help with. The brief states this rather than
  reranking the evidence to flatter the product.
- The saved library leaves v1 scope. This contradicts the capability list in
  `CLAUDE.md`, which needs a decision before it is edited.
- Clause coverage narrows to what job seekers actually face. Lease and
  freelance clause types are out of v1 tuning.
- If job seekers turn out not to pay, the finding is clean: it does not tell us
  tenants would not have. We are testing one segment, not the idea.
