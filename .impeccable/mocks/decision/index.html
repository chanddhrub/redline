Redline: four directions for the review screen

Each mock uses the synthetic Northwind offer letter from the demo. Click rows, cards and arrows: they open and close. Three rules hold in every option, because the product depends on them:

  • The contract's own words are always set in the serif (Tinos), and Redline's words never are. Readers can tell them apart without relying on colour.
  • Severity is always spelled out as a word, never shown by colour alone.
  • Only one finding is open at a time. The rest stay one line long, so the page never shows everything at once.
