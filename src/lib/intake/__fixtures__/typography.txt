Confidentiality and Invention Assignment — Rider B

1. Confidential information. You agree that the term “Confidential Information” means any information the Company designates as confidential, including the Company’s client lists, pricing, and unpublished ﬁnancial results.

2. Notice period. You must give the Company sixty (60) days’ written notice before resigning, and the Company may waive that notice at its sole discre-
tion.

3. Non-solicitation. For twelve (12) months after your last day you shall not solicit any employee of the Company, whether the solici­tation is direct or indirect.

4. Fees. The Company will reimburse your professional membership fees up to $1,200 per year, provided the receipts are ﬁled within thirty (30) days.

5. Survival. Sections 1 through 4 survive termination, including the non-
compete obligations described in your offer letter, and the conﬂict-of-interest policy.
