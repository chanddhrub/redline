## Per-Finding Detail

1. **Attorney contract review flat fees.** Standard contract review flat fees range $250-$650 typically (lawyers spend 2-3 hours); broader range $300-$3,000 depending on complexity. NDA review: $300-$800. Employment contract review: ~$420 flat. Master services agreement: ~$492 flat. Source: [ContractsCounsel — Lawyer Fees for Contract Review](https://www.contractscounsel.com/b/lawyer-fees-for-contract-review).

2. **Attorney hourly rates (2025/2026, Clio Legal Trends).** National average lawyer hourly rate is $349 (up 4% YoY). Corporate practice area highest at $461/hr; Juvenile lowest at $135/hr. D.C. highest by state at $492/hr; West Virginia lowest at $196/hr. Non-lawyer (paralegal) average $187/hr. Source: [Clio — Compare Average Lawyer Hourly Rate by State](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/compare-lawyer-rates/).

3. **Lease review costs for tenants.** Flat-fee lease review averages $450 (rental lease) to $660 (lease agreement generally); hourly $150-$400, with lease specialists at $200-$350/hr. Over half of landlord-tenant lawyers offer a free ~30-min consult; bar referral consults can be as low as $35 for 30 minutes. Source: [ContractsCounsel — Rental Lease Agreement Cost](https://www.contractscounsel.com/b/rental-lease-agreement-cost), [ContractsCounsel — Lease Agreement Cost](https://www.contractscounsel.com/b/lease-agreement-cost).

4. **Non-compete/employment contract review.** Average project cost $542.59; some flat-fee non-compete/non-solicit reviews priced at $1,000. Framed by lawyers as "modest compared to potential risks" — i.e., the price anchor people are told to accept is $500-$1,000 for a single document. Source: [ContractsCounsel — Non-Compete Agreement Cost](https://www.contractscounsel.com/b/non-compete-agreement-cost).

5. **Startup/SAFE review.** Flat-fee SAFE review ~$500, drafting ~$600; more complex SAFE + side letters $2,500-$5,000; first-year total startup legal spend $5,000-$15,000; hourly $400-$1,200 at top firms. Source: [ContractsCounsel — SAFE Agreement Cost](https://www.contractscounsel.com/b/safe-agreement-cost).

6. **Legal Services Corporation Justice Gap (2022).** Low-income Americans get no or inadequate help for 92% of their substantial civil legal problems. 74% of low-income households had 1+ civil legal problem in the past year; 39% had 5+. Among those who didn't seek help, 46% cited financial concerns and 53% doubted they could find a lawyer within budget — direct evidence of an affordability-driven "just deal with it" gap, though this report covers civil legal needs broadly, not contract review specifically. Source: [LSC — Low-Income Americans Face Immense Justice Gap](https://www.lsc.gov/press-release/low-income-americans-face-immense-justice-gap-according-new-legal-services-corporation-report), [Justice Gap Report — Executive Summary](https://justicegap.lsc.gov/resource/executive-summary/).

7. **"Just sign it" / non-reading behavior.** 91% of consumers accept ToS without reading (Deloitte, 2017 UK survey), rising to 97% among 18-34 year-olds. Separately, Brookings survey found roughly three-quarters of online users rarely read business terms of service. This is strong evidence of the behavior Redline targets, but it is evidence of avoidance, not of willingness to pay to fix it. Source: [Deloitte UK Design Blog via Medium](https://medium.com/deloitte-uk-design-blog/dont-read-t-cs-read-war-and-peace-981e8159529e), [Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/brookings-survey-finds-three-quarters-of-online-users-rarely-read-business-terms-of-service/).

8. **Adopted low-cost/subscription alternatives (revealed price points).** Rocket Lawyer: $39.99-$40/month subscription includes an AI contract-review copilot on all tiers, standard tier priced at $149. LegalZoom: attorney-consult subscription add-on renews at $49/month after a 30-day trial embedded in a $249 formation package. LegalShield: personal plans $29.95/month ($24.95 in MA), small-business plans $49/$99/$169/month, unlimited personal-document legal review (with per-document page limits). New AI-native entrants: QwickContractReview.com at $99 flat (later $19 promotional tier); ContractClarifyAI at $9 one-time or $29/month subscription. These are the closest real-market analogs to Redline's product and price range. Sources: [Rocket Lawyer vs LegalZoom comparison](https://www.llcuniversity.com/rocket-lawyer-vs-legalzoom/), [LegalShield pricing](https://www.legalshield.com/pricing), [QwickContractReview coverage](https://markets.financialcontent.com/clarkebroadcasting.mymotherlode/article/247pressrelease-2025-10-2-qwickcontractreviewcom-delivers-99-contract-reviews-in-48-hours-empowering-small-businesses-and-freelancers-nationwide), [ContractClarifyAI](https://alternativeto.net/software/contractclarifyai/about).
